Controls
  • W/↑ accelerate · S/↓ brake & reverse
  • A D or ← → steer
  • Shift or Space drift — release for a mini‑turbo
  • E use your item (from ? boxes: boost, shield, laser, cone, mud)
  • R reset to track · C camera · Esc pause
  • Gamepad: left stick steer, RT gas, LT brake, A drift, LB/RB item

Clips never leave your machine — they are read straight from the folder by your browser. Works best in Chrome or Edge (folder picker); other browsers use a standard file dialog.
